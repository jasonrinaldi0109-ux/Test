How the editor works

studio/editor.py
1 · Pick upNew videos in your Dropbox root are pulled into the studio and logged.
2 · ReframeMotion tracking follows the action and crops 16:9 gym footage to 9:16, smoothed like a camera operator.
3 · ScriptEach script is a list of shots: clip, timecode, on-screen text, voiceover line, CTA end card.
4 · Voice & masterDrop <script-id>.m4a in Dropbox. The editor adds subtitles, ducks the gym sound and masters to −14 LUFS.
